# Ashish Sharma Poetic Saga

A professional and responsive poetry and literary website showcasing the creative journey, poetry, books, achievements, awards, recognitions, and environmental work of **Ashish Sharma**, a World Record Holder Eco Poet.

## About

**Ashish Sharma Poetic Saga** is a dedicated literary website created to showcase Ashish Sharma's journey as a poet and environmental enthusiast.

The website highlights his poetry, editorial works, published books, environmental poetry, literary events, awards, recognitions, media coverage, and memorable moments from his poetic journey.

One of the key highlights of the website is Ashish Sharma's work of **writing 100 poems on Environment in 100 days**, covering different aspects of nature and the environment.
